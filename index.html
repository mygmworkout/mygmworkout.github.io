    Pilates on the GM1 — the Free Lift workaround

    The automated Pilates tracks don't launch on a Gym Monster 1, so you recreate the reformer by hand. Open the workout menu and choose Free Lift, then switch the lifting mode from Standard to Constant (steady, reformer-like tension) or Chains. Hook the long extension straps and dual-layer loops into the bottom cable ports. For full-range moves like Leg Circles or the Hundred, pull the cables out a few feet first to establish your active tension zone, then dial the weight per side to match the spring your class calls for:

    Yellow light / support · 4–7 lb
    Blue medium · 10–12 lb
    Red heavy / base · 15–22 lb
